How the two weeks run

  1. Before you arrive

    A call to turn your spec into one a factory will take seriously. We list the FCC, tariff and US restricted-list questions your part raises, so you can take them to a lawyer before you commit.

  2. 1

    Week one

    Hosted meetings with makers who build your kind of part, with someone from our side in the room. Factory visits to the ones worth a second look.

  3. 2

    Week two

    Go back to the best ones. Order samples if you choose to. Look for a second source.

  4. Every day

    A desk at a coworking space, next to other teams working on their own parts.

The plan is a starting point. We shape it around your part.
