Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Delta & Bay is a robotics consultancy in San Francisco and Shenzhen. This page explains what we collect when you send us a request, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

When you send the request form, we keep what you enter: your name, your email and WhatsApp number if you give them, what you're working on, what you need, any notes, when you need it and who would pay. We also record when you sent it, the page you sent it from and your browser type. The form then opens WhatsApp or your mail app with the same message, and we receive whatever you send us there.

If you applied for our January 2027 trip, we also hold the answers you gave on the application form, as described when you applied.

We haven't added advertising trackers or analytics tools to this site. Our hosting and database providers receive technical information, such as your IP address and request details, when your browser connects to their services.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and work out whether and how we can help.
  • To agree and carry out any work you ask us to do.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising, and there's no mailing list. We won't share what you're building with makers, labs or suppliers without asking you first.

Where it's kept

Requests and applications are stored in databases run by Supabase, hosted in the United States. Access is restricted to authorised members of the Delta & Bay team. We use Resend to email each new request to our team. The site is hosted by Vercel, and this privacy page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Messages you send on WhatsApp or by email are kept in those services.

How long we keep it

We keep a request while we're talking to you or working with you, and delete it within 12 months of our last contact. Trip applications are deleted within 12 months after the trip ends.

Your choices

To request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, email info@deltaandbay.com. We handle requests within the time limits required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions, which we'll explain if they apply.
