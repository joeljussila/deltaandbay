What the 10 days include

A room and a desk

Accommodation for the 9 nights, and a desk at SCI you can use all day, every day, with the other teams around you.

  • Supplier contacts

    We have supplier contacts across cameras, actuators, robot hands, arms and PCBs, and introduce you around your part. On supplier day, Sat 16, we book one-on-ones for you.

  • Optional visits

    Factories in Bao’an and Longgang, a day at XbotPark in Songshan Lake and a morning in Huaqiangbei. Join the ones that help your build.

  • Demo day, Tue 19

    Show what you built to the cohort and to founders and makers from Shenzhen.

  • Two big dinners

    A welcome dinner with a guest talk on Tue 12, and a goodbye dinner after demo day.

The 10 days

Most of the 10 days are yours. Build, and reach out to suppliers as much as you can: we help with introductions, translation and parts. Visits and talks are optional, so skip any that don’t help your build. Only four things are fixed: the welcome dinner, the supplier meetings, demo day and the goodbye dinner.

Where you’ll build

Our venue is secured: SCI Sino-Canadian International Innovation Centre, in Nanshan’s Science and Technology Park.

SCI is an ideal base for a hardware sprint. It puts you close to Shenzhen’s engineering talent and hardware businesses, with Bao’an’s manufacturing clusters and Huaqiangbei’s component markets within reach.

Teams can combine workshops with supplier visits and hands-on sourcing, turning design discussions into working prototypes and relationships with the people who can manufacture them.

Questions

Who can apply?

Hardware and robotics founders at any stage, from pre-idea to shipping, as long as you have something to build by January.

Is this your first sprint?

Yes. This is our founding cohort: 40 places, and it runs if 20 people have paid deposits by 15 December.

Who else is coming?

Hardware and robotics founders from San Francisco. Once places are confirmed, we introduce everyone, with permission, before January.

Which companies will we visit?

It depends on the parts the cohort is building. We confirm visits in writing once we know your part, and we don’t name a company before it has agreed.

Where do I stay, and how do I get around?

Accommodation is included for the 9 nights, close to SCI. Transport isn’t included: rides and the metro are cheap and easy, and we help you set them up on day one.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport, so check the official sources, such as the Chinese embassy or consulate where you live. Getting the right visa is your responsibility, and we don’t arrange visas or invitation letters. Apply early.

I don’t speak Chinese.

You don’t need to. Someone from our side joins the supplier meetings we set up, to translate.

Is it okay to buy from China under US rules?

For most parts, yes. Whole mobile robots are the main exception. We talk it through once we know your part.

What if I change my mind?

Your deposit is refundable for any reason until Tue 15 Dec. After that there are no refunds, unless the sprint doesn’t run, or we change the dates, the price or what’s included. Booking and refunds has the details.

Can’t make January?

Tell us your dates and what you’re building. If we have space and the right makers, we can host you. Tell us your dates.
