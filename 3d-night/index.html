The trip

Shenzhen brings together robotics companies, electronics suppliers and manufacturers. In five days you see the humanoid makers, the companies that build the joints, hands and circuit boards inside them, and the factory floors where it all scales. You meet Shenzhen's hardware founders, and you bring your own parts list to the Huaqiangbei electronics market.

Arrive Monday, fly home Friday evening, back by the weekend.

FAQ

Why do I have to apply?

The group is 10 to 20 people, and everyone should get as much from each other as from the visits. Your application also tells us what you're building, so we can match visits and supplier meetings to it.

Who are you looking for?

Founders choosing suppliers in the next six months, and investors active in robotics and hardware. We don't take defence companies.

What is the interview?

A 15-minute call about what you're building and what you want from the five days. We decide within 2 days and let you know either way.

When do I pay?

Nothing when you apply. If you're offered a place, we send an invoice for a $500 deposit, due within 7 days, which holds your place and your price. The balance is due by 11 December. The deposit isn't refundable if you cancel, but you can pass your place to someone else. If fewer than 10 people have paid in full by 11 December, the trip doesn't run and everyone gets a full refund, deposit included.

When should I book flights?

After we confirm the trip on 11 December. Fly into Shenzhen Bao'an (SZX) on 11 January, where we pick you up, and book a flight home that leaves after about 6pm on 15 January. If you fly through Hong Kong, we send directions for the border crossing.

Do I need a visa?

Entry rules depend on your passport and route. Check China's official entry requirements for your January 2027 travel dates before booking. Eligible US passport holders may qualify for 240-hour visa-free transit with a confirmed onward ticket to a third country or region. A US–China–US round trip does not meet that transit condition. We provide invitation letters if you need a visa.

Is it only for engineers?

No. Founders and investors from every background join; you just need a reason to be in Shenzhen.

Will there be translation?

Yes. A bilingual coordinator travels with the group and translates in every meeting.

Can I bring samples or drawings?

Yes, and they make supplier meetings more useful. Tell us in advance so we can look for a fit.
