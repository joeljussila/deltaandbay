What the 10 days include

The 10 days

Most of the 10 days are yours. Build, and reach out to suppliers as much as you can: we help with introductions, translation and parts. The outings are optional, and only the two dinners and demo day are fixed.

Questions

Who can apply?

Hardware and robotics founders at any stage, from pre-idea to shipping, as long as you have something to build by January.

Is this your first sprint?

Yes. This is our founding cohort: 40 places, and it runs once 20 are confirmed.

Who else is coming?

Hardware and robotics founders from San Francisco. Once places are confirmed, we introduce everyone, with permission, before January.

Which companies will we visit?

It depends on the parts the cohort is building. We confirm visits in writing once we know your part, and we don’t name a company before it has agreed.

Where do I stay, and how do I get around?

Accommodation is included for the 9 nights, close to the coworking space. Transport isn’t included: rides and the metro are cheap and easy, and we help you set them up on day one.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport. Once you’re accepted, we tell you exactly what you need and when to apply.

I don’t speak Chinese.

You don’t need to. Someone from our side is in your supplier meetings.

Is it okay to buy from China under US rules?

For most parts, yes. Whole mobile robots are the main exception. We talk it through once we know your part.

What if I change my mind?

Within 14 days of paying the deposit, you get it back in full. After that you can give your place to a teammate or another founder at no cost. Booking and refunds has the details.

Can’t make January?

Tell us your dates and what you’re building. If we have space and the right makers, we can host you. Tell us your dates.
